# Parent guide — how to run this practice

This is a ten-minute parent-led activity. You read the script; the learner works on the learner
page. The answer guide is for you and stays out of the learner's view.

## Before you start

- A measuring jug with 50 ml marks, water, and a towel.
- Two small glasses so "two batches" is something the learner can pour.

## Script

1. "Today we are measuring. Read Task 1 and show me where the liquid is on the jug."
2. Let the learner count the marks aloud. Do not correct the first attempt; note what happened.
3. "Now Task 2. One batch is 175 ml. We are making two batches. Show me how you get the answer."
4. Ask for the explanation in Task 3 in the learner's own words.
5. If the learner is stuck, point at the 50 ml marks and ask them to count with you. That is
   guidance, and guided work is recorded as guided, not as independent evidence.

## Recording

Write one line after the session: what the learner did, what help you gave, and what you would
change next time. Do not write a grade. Keep the note in your own records; it is not part of a
shared pack.

## Planning note

This activity was prepared before the session and approved by you as the parent. It was not
generated during the lesson. Changing the quantities or the activity invalidates that approval
and needs a fresh review.
