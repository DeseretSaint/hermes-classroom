# Answer guide — parent copy

Keep this page out of the learner's view. It is the answer key and the observation record.

## Task 1 — read the jug

**150 ml.** The marks are every 50 ml: 50, 100, 150. The liquid reaches the third mark.

## Task 2 — double the recipe

**350 ml.** One batch is 175 ml, so two batches are 175 + 175 = 350 ml.

## Task 3 — explain the working

A complete explanation names both quantities and the operation: "one batch is 175 ml, so two
batches are 350 ml because I added 175 and 175."

## Observation checklist

- Reads the scale correctly (counts marks rather than estimating).
- Doubles a quantity and can say why addition is the right operation.
- Uses the words "one batch" and "two batches" in the explanation.
- If the learner multiplies by two, accept it — the evidence target is the reasoning, not the
  method.
- If the learner answers 200 ml, ask them to point at the marks on the jug. That is a scale
  reading error, not a careless one; re-teach with a drawn scale before retrying.

## What this record is

This is a parent-reported observation of one practice session. It is not a diagnosis, a grade
point, or a claim that the skill is mastered. A second session with a different jug and different
quantities is the next evidence.
