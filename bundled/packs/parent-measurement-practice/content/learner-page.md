# Measuring ingredients

You are helping make a fruit drink for four people. Read each task and write your answer on the
line. Show your working next to the line if it helps.

## Task 1 — read the jug

The measuring jug has marks every 50 ml. The liquid reaches the mark after 100 ml, then one more
mark.

How much liquid is in the jug? ____________ ml

## Task 2 — double the recipe

The recipe uses 175 ml of juice for one batch. You are making two batches of the same size.

How much juice do you need for two batches? ____________ ml

## Task 3 — check your answer

Explain to your parent how you worked out Task 2. Use the words "one batch" and "two batches" in
your explanation.
