The greeting in kana and romaji

Example
こんにちは (konnichiwa) is the standard daytime greeting in Japanese. It is written こ・ん・に・ち・は; in this fixed greeting the final は is pronounced wa. Say it evenly: ko-n-ni-chi-wa.

When to say it

Explain
Say it when you enter a shop or meet someone during the day. It opens the interaction; it is neither a request nor a farewell.

Say it back

Practice
Play the pronunciation reference and repeat こんにちは three times, matching the rhythm ko-n-ni-chi-wa. The recording is the model; your own saying is the practice, and this page does not assess pronunciation.
