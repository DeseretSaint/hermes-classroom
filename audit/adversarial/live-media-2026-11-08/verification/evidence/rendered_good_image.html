The bench, described

Example
The wheel is clamped upright in the stand. The torque wrench lies on the bench beside it, within reach and not on the floor.

Why the layout matters

Explain
A clamped wheel stays still while both hands work, and a wrench within reach is not left on the floor where it can be kicked or lost.

Check your own bench

Practice
Look at the illustration, then at your own bench: is the wheel clamped, and is the wrench beside it? Name anything that is not in place before you start.

Where should the torque wrench be before you start?

Example
Look at the bench, not the floor Find the tool within reach of the clamped wheel Answer: on the bench beside the clamped wheel

Recap

Recap
The illustration is context, not evidence: it shows the layout the procedure assumes. It carries no measurements and no torque values.
