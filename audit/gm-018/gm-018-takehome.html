Parent guide - Section 1: the sear

Objective: Objective 1: sear and braise Evidence target: production

Course goal: Braise short ribs without a pressure cooker.

Lesson intent

Sear first, then braise.

Materials and preparation

  • A heavy pan
  • Tongs
  • Neutral oil

What the learner will do

Choose the moment that actually sears the meat.

Worked example

The pan is only warm.

  1. Wait until the oil shimmers
  2. Lay the ribs away from you

Parent note

Ask why a cold pan cannot sear.

Supervision

  • Use the back burner

Narration script

Browning builds the flavour the braise carries for two hours. Watch the oil, then lay the ribs away from you.

Success criterion

Browns the meat on every face
